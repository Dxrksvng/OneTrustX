3Proposed Solution

One TrustX: baggage disruption intelligence

Combines baggage, flight, resource and disruption signals to detect bottlenecks early, predict downstream impact and recommend recovery priorities — the operator decides.

Data Integration

Scans, flight schedule and staffing become one event format.

Trusted Event History

Every record has a source, a time and a freshness check — auditable.

Common Operational State

One shared status of the six baggage stages.

AI Decision Intelligence

Detect bottleneck · Predict at-risk flights · Recommend order · Coordinate owners
Overlay, not replacement

We don't build another baggage tracker

BRS already tracks bags. We take signals from BRS, AODB and ground teams and work out where it is stuck, what it affects and what to do first.
Four questions, one screen
① Is it normal?
② Which stage is stuck?
③ Which flights, how many bags?
④ What first — and by when?
!Same four layers as One TrustX in round one, applied to baggage data. The system recommends; people decide; no airport equipment is controlled.3 / 8
