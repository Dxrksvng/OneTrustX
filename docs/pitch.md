# Three-minute pitch

0:00–0:25 — Problem: Passengers face uncertainty accumulated across check-in, screening, immigration, walking and boarding. This is a problem statement, not a measured claim about a named airport.

0:25–0:45 — Insight: Airports increasingly have operational intelligence. Choosing an intervention and estimating its network consequence is a distinct decision problem. One TrustX can sit above existing infrastructure; it does not claim to replace SKY Smart Flows.

0:45–1:10 — Observe and predict: Reset the illustrative terminal, then trigger a synthetic departure wave. Show Security A's current 12 minutes and +30 forecast of 39, with labelled assumption bands. Explain incoming demand versus available service capacity.

1:10–1:55 — Simulate and decide: Compare four options. Opening a lane uses staff; redirecting passengers adds walking and affects Security B. Show calculated P95, passenger-minutes saved, constraints and transparent score. Accept the combined option with a human reason. The demo action changes a model, not airport equipment.

1:55–2:25 — Passenger action: Reset the passenger vignette. Gate C8 changes to E7; accessible Safe Time falls 34→11. Show the revised route and Proceed now message.

2:25–2:45 — Measure and learn: Show predicted versus synthetic observed wait in the decision log. Confidence is not a guarantee; stale inputs block actions. Explain why the model still needs independent airport validation.

2:45–3:00 — Close: “Today airport systems tell us what is happening. One TrustX helps us decide what to do before it happens.”

Keep the simulated-result label on screen. Report only the live calculated demo metrics, never a claimed business saving or actual airport performance improvement.
